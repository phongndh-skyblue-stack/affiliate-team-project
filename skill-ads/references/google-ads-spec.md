# Google Ads Responsive Search Ads (RSA) & Ad Extensions Specifications

This document contains standard Google Ads specifications to help verify the accuracy of headlines, descriptions, and ad extensions when writing sample ad copy.

## 1. Responsive Search Ads (RSA)
- **Maximum number of headlines:** 15 headlines.
- **Headline character limit:** Maximum 30 characters (using standard UTF-8 characters).
- **Maximum number of descriptions:** 4 descriptions.
- **Description character limit:** Maximum 90 characters.

## 2. Callout Extensions
- **Purpose:** Highlight free services, promotions, and exclusive features (e.g., Free Shipping, 24/7 Support).
- **Recommended quantity:** At least 4 displayed callouts.
- **Character limit:** Maximum 25 characters per callout.

## 3. Sitelink Extensions
- **Purpose:** Direct customers to specific pages on the website (e.g., Contact page, Features page, Promotions).
- **Recommended quantity:** At least 4 sitelinks.
- **Structure of each sitelink:**
  - **Sitelink text (Title):** Maximum 25 characters.
  - **Description line 1:** Maximum 35 characters.
  - **Description line 2:** Maximum 35 characters.
  - **Final URL:** The actual URL or relative URL leading to the corresponding feature page.

## 4. Google Ads Content Policy (Summary)
- Do not capitalize entire words (e.g., DO NOT WRITE LIKE THIS).
- Do not repeat punctuation marks or use strange characters (e.g., Buy Now!!!).
- Avoid sensitive terms that are automatically scanned and flagged, such as "NSFW", "adult chat", "gambling" in the ad copy. Replace them with metaphorical terms, euphemisms, or safer alternatives.
